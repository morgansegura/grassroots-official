## What we ARE doing — the 3 priority channels

These are the highest yield per hour at part-time capacity.

### Channel 1 — SDFC + San Diego Foundation pool

**Why:** SDFC seeded $100K at SDF specifically for "underserved San Diego County, removing barriers to participation." That's your use case verbatim — you're applying to a fund that's already named for your work.

**Action plan:**

1. Pitch SDFC community/foundation team directly (intro email + 30-min meeting)
2. Apply through San Diego Foundation for distribution from the SDFC pool
3. Reference both in any follow-up grant applications elsewhere — pro-club partnership is a strong validation signal

**Time budget:** 10–15 hours over 6–8 weeks
**Y1 yield estimate:** $10K–$50K
**Owner:** Morgan (relationship-driven)

### Channel 2 — Local corporate sponsors (2–3 deals)

**Why:** Local SD businesses sponsor youth teams routinely. World Cup '26 hook + Latino-community framing gives the pitch a story. Each landed sponsor unlocks the next two.

**Target list:**

- Sycuan Tribe philanthropic giving
- Viejas philanthropic giving
- Sempra Energy CSR
- Qualcomm Foundation
- Sharp / Scripps community giving
- Larger local family-business owners with kids in club soccer (sponsorship of a team is emotionally resonant)

**Pitch frame:** "Sponsor a full team for the Class of '26 — $25K = 16-20 kids funded for one season. Your logo on the roster page, recognition at our launch clinic, named team sponsor in annual report. Pre-2026-WC brand moment."

**Action plan:**

1. Build 1-page sponsor deck (or use the donor one-pager + add sponsor benefits)
2. List 10–15 target companies
3. Find warm intros where possible (LinkedIn, parent network — your soccer-cost-wall network IS useful here as an intro pipeline even if they're not direct donors)
4. Pitch 2–3 conversations a month for 6 months
5. Close 2–3 sponsors in Y1

**Time budget:** 30–40 hours over 6–9 months
**Y1 yield estimate:** $15K–$50K
**Owner:** Both founders (relationship-driven, but Mirella's Latino/business network may carry weight in tribal and Latino-business outreach)

### Channel 3 — Foundation grants (start small, build track record)

**Why:** Small grants are scoped exactly for your stage. LA84 is SD-eligible and rolling — no deadline pressure. Each landed grant is a credibility signal for the next.

**Y1 sequence:**

1. **Good Sports** (in-kind equipment) — apply this month. Easy win.
2. **LA84 Foundation Small Grant** — $5K–$25K, rolling. Apply when one signed sponsor or major donor is in place.
3. **San Diego Foundation** — when SDFC pool conversation matures.
4. **U.S. Soccer Foundation / Soccer Forward** — watch for next Innovate to Grow cycle.

**Time budget:** 25–40 hours over 9 months (mostly LOI/proposal writing; reuse boilerplate)
**Y1 yield estimate:** $15K–$40K
**Owner:** Morgan + Claude drafting

## What we are NOT doing (and why)

| Not doing                                 | Why                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Paid digital ads (Meta, Google)           | Cost-per-dollar-raised is $0.50–$2.00 at our scale. Net-negative.                                            |
| Galas / events                            | Year 1 events lose money at our infrastructure level. Maybe Y2.                                              |
| Daily social media content production     | Time cost too high for our hours. Maybe one post a week max.                                                 |
| Cold outbound at scale                    | Personal time spent on outreach has 10x higher yield.                                                        |
| 5+ simultaneous grant applications        | Each one is 4–8 hours; quality > quantity.                                                                   |
| Civic club tour (Rotary, Kiwanis circuit) | Each meeting is 2–3 hours including travel/prep; yields modest. Maybe Y2.                                    |
| Major fundraising hire                    | We can't afford it Y1; we'd be paying for capacity we don't have revenue to support.                         |
| Press chasing                             | One earned media story is great; chasing 10 reporters is time-expensive. Let press come from one good story. |
