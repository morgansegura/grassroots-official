# Board Member — Grassroots Foundation

> One page to share with a prospective board member.
> Grass Roots Foundation (dba Grassroots Foundation) · 501(c)(3) · EIN 35-2822183 · San Diego

## What we do

Grassroots Foundation helps kids in San Diego play club soccer when their families can't afford the fees, uniforms, and gear. A single season can run $2,500 or more, and too many talented kids get left out for one reason: money. We cover those costs so they can play.

## What we're asking of you

We're building our founding board: a small group who believe in this and will help steer it well. The honest commitment:

- **Time:** about 4–6 board meetings a year (1–2 hours each), plus occasional input by phone or email. Roughly 30–40 hours a year.
- **Governance:** help make the big calls. Approve the budget, keep us accountable to our mission and the law, and review how we operate.
- **Paperwork:** sign a short annual conflict-of-interest disclosure (standard for every nonprofit board).
- **Help where you can:** an introduction, a door opened, or support with a fundraising ask once or twice a year. We'll never put you on the spot.

## What we're NOT asking

- You're not personally on the hook. Directors of California 501(c)(3) public charities are protected by the state's volunteer-immunity law, and the foundation carries Directors & Officers insurance.
- It's a volunteer role, unpaid, like all of ours.

## Officer roles (if you're open to one)

- **Treasurer** — financial oversight; reviews the books and the annual filing. Ideal for a finance background.
- **Secretary** — keeps the meeting minutes and corporate records.
- Or simply serve as a **Director** — a voice and a vote.

## Why you

We don't ask people lightly. We're asking because we trust your judgment and what you'd bring to the room. If you say yes, you'll help decide how a kid in San Diego gets to keep playing the game they love.

Thank you for considering it.

— Morgan Segura, Founder
