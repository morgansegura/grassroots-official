# Scholarship templates

Fill-in-the-blank companions to `scholarship-sop.md`. Keep the wording — it is
written so a family reading it does not feel means-tested, and so an examiner
reading it sees criteria rather than discretion.

Last updated: August 19, 2026

---

## 1. Conflict-of-interest disclosure

One per person, per round. File with the round's minutes. **Everyone submits
one, including a "no conflicts" version** — a disclosure only appearing when
there is something to disclose tells you who has a conflict just by existing.

> **Grassroots Foundation — Conflict Disclosure**
> Round: `____________` Date: `____________`
> Name: `____________` Role in this round: `____________`
>
> I have reviewed the applicant and player names in this round.
>
> ☐ I have **no** personal relationship with any applicant family.
> ☐ I have a relationship with the following applicant(s), described here:
> `______________________________________________`
> Nature of the relationship (teammate of my child, family friend, neighbor,
> employer, club colleague, other): `______________________`
>
> If I declared a relationship, I understand that I will not score any
> application in this round, will not advocate for any applicant formally or
> informally, and will not be present for the decision.
>
> Signed: `____________`

## 3. Award notice to the club

> Subject: Grassroots Foundation scholarship — [Player name], [Season]
>
> Hello [Name],
>
> Grassroots Foundation has awarded a need-based scholarship of **$[amount]**
> toward [Player name]'s [season] participation at [Club].
>
> Payment goes directly to [Club], not to the family. Please send an invoice
> referencing the player and season to [email], and apply the credit to the
> family's balance.
>
> If [Player name] leaves the program during the season, please let us know so
> we can discuss the unused balance — it stays within the program.
>
> Thank you for the confirmation you provided during review, and for the work
> you do for these families.
>
> [Name], Grassroots Foundation

## 4. Applicant — approved

> Subject: Your Grassroots Foundation scholarship application
>
> Hello [Name],
>
> Good news — we are able to help. Grassroots Foundation has awarded **$[amount]**
> toward [Player name]'s [season] with [Club].
>
> **You do not need to do anything.** We pay [Club] directly and the credit
> will appear on your family's balance. If you have questions about the
> remaining balance, [Club] is the right contact.
>
> If your family's circumstances change in either direction, tell us. We would
> rather adjust than have you drop out quietly.
>
> Applications reopen for [next season]. Scholarships are not automatic — you
> will need to apply again, and we will tell you when the window opens.
>
> Thank you for trusting us with your information.
>
> [Name], Grassroots Foundation

## 5. Applicant — waitlisted

Say plainly that they qualified. This is the message families remember.

> Subject: Your Grassroots Foundation scholarship application
>
> Hello [Name],
>
> **Your application qualified.** We were not able to fund it this round —
> we committed the full amount we had, and requests came to more than our
> available funds.
>
> That is not a judgment about your family or [Player name]. You are on our
> waitlist, which means:
>
> - If funds free up this season, we come back to your application first, in
>   order.
> - When the next round opens we will email you directly so you do not have to
>   watch for it.
>
> If your club has its own financial assistance, it is worth asking them as
> well — many do, and the two can stack.
>
> We are sorry not to have better news this time.
>
> [Name], Grassroots Foundation

## 6. Applicant — declined as ineligible

> Subject: Your Grassroots Foundation scholarship application
>
> Hello [Name],
>
> Thank you for applying. We are not able to move your application forward this
> round, because [specific reason — e.g. the program is outside San Diego
> > County / the costs described fall outside what our scholarships cover].
>
> If that is wrong or your situation has changed, reply and tell us — we would
> rather re-read an application than lose a family over a detail.
>
> [If applicable:] You may also want to contact [Club] directly about their own
> financial assistance.
>
> [Name], Grassroots Foundation

## 7. Club confirmation request

> Subject: Scholarship review — confirming costs for [Player name]
>
> Hello [Name],
>
> A family at [Club] has applied to Grassroots Foundation for a need-based
> scholarship, and gave us permission to contact you. To finish the review we
> need three things:
>
> 1. Is [Player name] enrolled, or able to be enrolled, for [season]?
> 2. What is the actual annual cost for their team — tuition, kit, and any
>    required fees?
> 3. Has the family already paid part of it?
>
> We do not need financial documents from you or from the family, and we are
> not asking you to assess need — just to confirm enrollment and cost.
>
> If [Club] runs its own assistance and is already covering part of this,
> please say so, so we do not duplicate.
>
> Thank you,
> [Name], Grassroots Foundation
